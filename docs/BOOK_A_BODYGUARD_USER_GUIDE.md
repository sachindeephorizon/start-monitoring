## Book a Bodyguard — How It Works

**Document purpose:** This guide explains the end-to-end process of booking a bodyguard inside the DeepHorizon Security app, what information you will be asked to provide, what happens after you submit a booking, and how to view or cancel a booking.

---

## Overview

The **Book a Bodyguard** feature allows eligible users to request professional bodyguard services for a selected date and location. After you submit your request, your booking is recorded and reviewed for fulfillment. You will receive updates as the booking status changes.

---

## Eligibility and access

The Book a Bodyguard feature is available to users who have:

- **An active subscription**, or
- **An active trial**, where applicable.

If your access is not active, the app will prompt you to subscribe before you can place a booking.

---

## What you will be asked to provide

To place a booking, you will complete a short form with the following details:

- **City (Required):** The city where you need bodyguard services.
- **Reason for booking (Required):** A brief description of your requirement and context.
- **Number of bodyguards (Required):** The number of personnel you want to request.
- **Date (Required):** The date you want the service.

**Important:** At this time, the service schedule is set as:

- **Start time:** 9:00 AM (local time) on the selected date  
- **Duration:** 8 hours (standard shift)

---

## Step-by-step booking process

### 1) Open the booking form

1. Open the app and go to the **Home** screen.
2. Select **Book a Bodyguard**.

The booking form will open as a secure in-app modal.

### 2) Complete the booking form

Enter the required details:

- Choose or type your **city**.
- Provide a clear **reason** for the request (helpful for planning and assignment).
- Select the **number of bodyguards** you require.
- Choose the **date** of service.

### 3) Confirm the booking

After you confirm:

- The app validates the details.
- Your booking request is submitted and recorded.
- You will see a confirmation message containing your **Booking ID** and summary details.

---

## What happens after you submit a booking

After submission, your booking enters a processing workflow. The app will reflect updates as the booking status changes.

### Booking statuses

Your booking may show one of the following statuses:

- **Pending:** Your request has been received and is awaiting confirmation.
- **Confirmed:** Your request has been accepted and scheduled.
- **Active:** The service window has started.
- **Completed:** The service window has ended.
- **Cancelled:** The booking was cancelled (by you or by operational handling when applicable).

---

## Notifications and updates

You may receive **in-app notifications** (and, if enabled, push notifications) when:

- A booking is confirmed
- A booking status changes
- A booking is cancelled or completed

**Note for testing:** Push notifications are not available on iOS simulators; use a physical device to test push delivery.

---

## Viewing your bookings

You can view bodyguard bookings in your activity history:

1. Open **History**.
2. Look for entries related to **Bodyguard** services.

Each entry includes key details such as the date, city, and current status.

---

## Cancelling a booking

### When cancellation is allowed

Bookings can be cancelled only when the booking is in:

- **Pending**, or
- **Confirmed**

Once a booking is **Active**, cancellation may not be available inside the app.

### How to cancel

If cancellation is available for your booking, select the booking and choose **Cancel**. The status will update to **Cancelled**.

---

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling is not currently supported as an in-app action. If you need to change the booking date or details:

- **Cancel the existing booking** (if eligible), and
- **Create a new booking** with the correct information.

---

## Pricing and service notes

- **Standard shift reference:** 8 hours  
- **Indicative starting rate:** ₹3500 for an 8-hour shift  

Final pricing may vary depending on factors such as location, number of bodyguards, and event requirements. For bulk bookings or events, special pricing may be available.

---

## Safety and compliance

For user safety and service integrity:

- Bodyguard services are intended for legitimate personal safety needs.
- The app may require complete and accurate booking details to proceed.

If additional verification or details are needed, you may be contacted through the information associated with your account.

---

## Current limitations (transparent summary)

At present, the Book a Bodyguard feature has the following limitations:

- **Fixed schedule:** 9:00 AM start with an 8-hour duration
- **City-based location only:** No GPS coordinates are required in the booking form
- **No reschedule action:** Cancel and rebook if changes are needed
- **Cancellation constraints:** Allowed only in Pending/Confirmed status
- **Access required:** Active subscription or trial access is required
